📚 LearnMate – Student Study Workspace

Your studies, organized in one place.

LearnMate is a student-focused study management platform designed to help university students organize their academic activities, improve productivity, and track their learning progress through a unified dashboard.

✨ Features

- 📊 Dashboard – Get an overview of daily study goals, pending assignments, upcoming exams, study streaks, and GPA.
- 📅 Weekly Timetable – Organize class schedules, lectures, labs, and seminars.
- 📝 Assignments & Coursework – Track assignment deadlines, priorities, submissions, and completion status.
- 📖 Smart Study Notes – Create, organize, search, and manage subject-wise notes.
- 🎯 Exam Revision Planner – Monitor exam countdowns and syllabus completion.
- 🧠 Interactive Revision Quizzes – Test your knowledge with subject-based quizzes of different difficulty levels.
- ⏱️ Study Planner & Focus Timer – Manage study sessions using a Pomodoro-style timer.
- 📈 Progress & Study Analytics – Visualize study hours, academic performance, and subject-wise progress.
- 👤 Profile & Preferences – Manage student information, academic details, and preferences.

🎨 UI & Design

- Clean and modern user interface.
- Calm cream-and-green colour palette.
- Card-based dashboard layout.
- Consistent navigation across modules.
- Responsive and student-friendly design.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of LearnMate is to provide university students with a centralized platform to manage their academic responsibilities efficiently.

Instead of switching between multiple applications, students can organize their schedules, assignments, notes, revision activities, and productivity in one workspace.
